📖 Story

Mario goes on exciting adventures to save Princess Peach from Bowser. Along the way he travels through different worlds, defeats enemies, collects coins, and gains special powers.

👥 Characters

The main characters include Mario, Luigi, Princess Peach, and Bowser. Each character has a unique personality and role in the Mario universe.

⭐ Review

Super Mario is one of the most successful video game franchises ever. Its fun gameplay, colorful worlds, and memorable characters make it a timeless classic enjoyed by millions of players.
